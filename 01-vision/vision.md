# Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision, strategic direction, business objectives, and product principles of Maroc Local Hub.

It serves as the foundation for every technical, design, and business decision throughout the platform lifecycle.

All future specifications must align with this vision.

---

# Product Vision

Maroc Local Hub is not simply an e-commerce platform.

It is the digital infrastructure that enables Moroccan cooperatives, artisans, farmers, and local producers to access global markets through a modern, intelligent, and immersive commerce experience.

The platform combines:

- Commerce
- Artificial Intelligence
- Digital Identity
- 3D Product Visualization
- Storytelling
- International Logistics

into one scalable ecosystem.

---

# Mission

Our mission is to empower Moroccan producers by removing technological and commercial barriers and providing access to a world-class digital marketplace.

The platform must allow every cooperative—regardless of size—to compete internationally using advanced technology.

---

# Vision Statement

Become the world's leading marketplace dedicated to authentic Moroccan products while setting a new standard for AI-powered commerce.

# Non-Goals

The platform will NOT become:

A social network.

A generic marketplace.

A low-cost template store.

A clone of existing marketplaces.

---

# Definition of Success

Maroc Local Hub succeeds when:

A cooperative from any Moroccan region can publish products within minutes.

A customer anywhere in the world can confidently discover, understand, and purchase authentic Moroccan products.

The experience is faster, simpler, and more trustworthy than competing platforms.
