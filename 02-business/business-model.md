# Purpose

This document defines the business model of Maroc Local Hub.

It explains how the platform creates value for customers, producers, cooperatives, partners, and investors while ensuring long-term sustainability.

---

# Executive Summary

Maroc Local Hub is a technology-driven marketplace that connects Moroccan producers directly with customers worldwide.

The platform generates revenue through multiple streams while helping local producers digitize their businesses and expand internationally.

# Business Success Criteria

The business model is considered successful when:

- The marketplace is financially sustainable.
- Producers increase their income.
- Customers trust the platform.
- International sales grow continuously.
- The platform becomes the primary destination for authentic Moroccan products.
