# About Page Specification

Document ID: MLH-PAGE-003
Version: 1.0.0
Status: Draft
Owner: Maroc Local Hub

## Purpose

This document defines the About Page for Maroc Local Hub.

The About Page introduces the platform, explains its mission, and builds trust with customers, sellers, cooperatives, artisans, and partners.

---

## Page Objectives

The About Page should:

- Explain what Maroc Local Hub is
- Present the platform mission
- Explain the platform vision
- Highlight Moroccan local products
- Promote cooperatives and local sellers
- Build trust with customers
- Explain the value of the platform

---

## Page Sections

The page should include:

1. Hero Section
2. About Maroc Local Hub
3. Our Mission
4. Our Vision
5. What We Offer
6. Local Cooperatives
7. Moroccan Artisans
8. Local Sellers
9. Customers
10. Trust and Quality
11. Call to Action

---

## Hero Section

The Hero Section should include:

- Main headline
- Short description
- Supporting image or visual
- Primary Call-to-Action
- Secondary Call-to-Action

Example headline:

Discover Morocco. Support Local.

Example description:

Maroc Local Hub connects customers around the world with Moroccan cooperatives, artisans, and local sellers.

---

## Our Mission

The mission of Maroc Local Hub is to connect Moroccan local producers, cooperatives, artisans, and sellers with customers in Morocco and international markets.

The platform aims to promote local products, support sustainable economic development, and create new digital opportunities for Moroccan businesses.

---

## Our Vision

Our vision is to build a trusted global digital marketplace that showcases the diversity, quality, and authenticity of Moroccan products.

---

## What We Offer

The platform provides:

- Local product discovery
- Online shopping
- Seller storefronts
- Cooperative visibility
- Product search
- Secure checkout
- Order management
- Multilingual experiences
- International market access

---

## Local Cooperatives

Maroc Local Hub helps Moroccan cooperatives present and promote their products online.

Cooperatives can:

- Create a seller profile
- Showcase their products
- Manage their store
- Reach new customers
- Promote their local identity

---

## Moroccan Artisans

The platform supports Moroccan artisans by providing digital visibility for traditional and handmade products.

---

## Local Sellers

Local sellers can use the platform to create online stores and reach customers through a modern digital marketplace.

---

## Customers

Customers can discover and purchase authentic Moroccan products from trusted local sellers and cooperatives.

---

## Trust and Quality

The platform should promote:

- Seller transparency
- Clear product information
- Authentic product presentation
- Customer reviews
- Secure transactions
- Reliable delivery information

---

## Call to Action

The page should provide clear actions for users.

Primary actions:

- Explore Products
- Browse Categories

Secondary actions:

- Become a Seller
- Join as a Cooperative

---

## Mobile Experience

The About Page must be optimized for mobile-first experiences.

Requirements:

- Responsive layout
- Fast loading
- Readable typography
- Optimized images
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Clear navigation

---

## RTL Support

The page must fully support Arabic RTL layouts.

All text, spacing, alignment, icons, and directional elements should adapt correctly to RTL.

---

## Localization

The page should support:

- Arabic
- French
- English
- Spanish

Content must be designed to accommodate different text lengths.

---

## Accessibility

The page must:

- Support keyboard navigation
- Support screen readers
- Use semantic HTML
- Provide accessible image descriptions
- Maintain sufficient color contrast
- Provide clear heading hierarchy

---

## Design Principles

The About Page should be:

- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Authentic
- Mobile-first
- Accessible
- Multilingual

---

## Conclusion

The About Page should communicate the purpose and values of Maroc Local Hub while building trust with customers, cooperatives, artisans, and sellers.

The page should encourage visitors to explore Moroccan products and participate in the local digital marketplace.
