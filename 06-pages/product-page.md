# Product Page

Document ID: MLH-PAGE-002

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Draft

Owner: Maroc Local Hub

## Purpose

This document defines the Product Detail Page.

The page should present complete product information and encourage purchase.

---

## Main Sections

- Product Images
- Product Title
- Price
- Rating
- Seller Information
- Product Description
- Specifications
- Reviews
- Related Products

---

## User Actions

Users should be able to:

- Add to Cart
- Buy Now
- Add to Wishlist
- Share Product
- Contact Seller

---

## Mobile Experience

The page must be optimized for smartphones.

---

## RTL Support

Support Arabic RTL layouts.

---

## Conclusion

The Product Page should help users make informed purchasing decisions.
