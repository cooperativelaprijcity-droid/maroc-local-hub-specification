# Purpose

This document defines the complete buyer journey on Maroc Local Hub.

The goal is to provide a simple, secure, and mobile-first shopping experience.

---

# Flow Overview

Buyer Journey:

Home Page
↓

Search Products
↓

Browse Categories
↓

Product Details
↓

Add to Cart
↓

Shopping Cart
↓

Checkout
↓

Payment
↓

Order Confirmation
↓

Track Order
↓

Leave Review

---

# Step 1: Home Page

The buyer lands on the Home Page.

Available actions:

- Search products
- Browse categories
- View promotions
- View featured products

---

# Step 2: Search

The buyer searches for products.

Features:

- Search suggestions
- Filters
- Sorting
- Voice search (future)

---

# Step 3: Product Details

The buyer views:

- Images
- Description
- Price
- Seller
- Reviews
- Availability

Available actions:

- Add to Cart
- Buy Now
- Add to Wishlist
- Contact Seller

---

# Step 4: Shopping Cart

Buyer reviews selected products.

Available actions:

- Change quantity
- Remove items
- Apply coupon
- Continue shopping

---

# Step 5: Checkout

Buyer enters:

- Shipping address
- Delivery method
- Payment method

System validates information before payment.

---

# Step 6: Payment

Supported payment methods:

- Credit Card
- PayPal
- Cash on Delivery
- Bank Transfer

---

# Step 7: Confirmation

Buyer receives:

- Order number
- Order summary
- Estimated delivery date

---

# Step 8: Order Tracking

Buyer can:

- Track shipment
- Contact seller
- Download invoice

---

# Step 9: Review

After delivery, buyer can:

- Rate product
- Leave review
- Report issues

---

# Mobile Requirements

The entire flow must be optimized for smartphones.

---

# RTL Support

Support Arabic RTL layouts.

---

# Accessibility

Support keyboard navigation and screen readers.

---

# Conclusion

The Buyer Flow should provide a fast, secure, and user-friendly purchasing experience.
