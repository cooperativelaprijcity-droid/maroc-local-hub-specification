# Purpose

This document defines the payment process for Maroc Local Hub.

The payment flow must provide a secure, reliable, and user-friendly experience for buyers and sellers.

---

# Payment Journey

Checkout
↓

Select Payment Method
↓

Validate Payment Information
↓

Process Payment
↓

Payment Confirmed
↓

Create Order
↓

Send Confirmation
↓

Notify Seller

---

# Supported Payment Methods

The platform should support:

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- PayPal
- Stripe
- Cash on Delivery
- Bank Transfer
- Mobile Wallet (Future)

---

# Payment Validation

The system shall verify:

- Payment method
- Payment amount
- Currency
- Transaction status
- Fraud indicators

---

# Payment Status

Supported statuses:

- Pending
- Authorized
- Paid
- Failed
- Cancelled
- Refunded

---

# Security

The payment system must:

- Use HTTPS
- Encrypt sensitive data
- Follow PCI-DSS best practices
- Prevent duplicate transactions
- Detect suspicious activity

---

# Notifications

Notify users when:

- Payment succeeds
- Payment fails
- Refund issued
- Payment cancelled

---

# Mobile Requirements

The payment flow must be optimized for smartphones.

---

# Accessibility

Support keyboard navigation and screen readers.

---

# RTL Support

Support Arabic RTL layouts.

---

# Localization

Supported languages:

- Arabic
- French
- English
- Spanish

---

# Conclusion

The payment flow should ensure secure, fast, and reliable transactions while protecting buyers and sellers.
