---

# Purpose

This document defines the refund process on Maroc Local Hub.

The refund flow ensures that eligible customers can request refunds while providing sellers with a fair review process.

---

# Refund Journey

Order Delivered
↓

Customer Requests Refund
↓

Refund Request Submitted
↓

Seller Reviews Request
↓

Platform Reviews (if required)
↓

Refund Approved or Rejected
↓

Customer Notified
↓

Payment Refunded

---

# Eligible Refund Reasons

Customers may request a refund for:

- Damaged Product
- Wrong Product
- Missing Items
- Product Not as Described
- Defective Product
- Order Not Delivered

---

# Refund Request

The customer should provide:

- Order Number
- Refund Reason
- Description
- Photos (Optional)
- Supporting Documents (Optional)

---

# Seller Actions

The seller can:

- Accept Refund
- Reject Refund
- Request More Information
- Offer Replacement

---

# Platform Actions

The platform may:

- Review evidence
- Contact buyer
- Contact seller
- Make a final decision
- Issue refund

---

# Refund Status

Supported statuses:

- Refund Requested
- Under Review
- Approved
- Rejected
- Refunded
- Closed

---

# Notifications

Notify users when:

- Refund Requested
- Additional Information Required
- Refund Approved
- Refund Rejected
- Refund Completed

---

# Security

The system must:

- Prevent fraudulent refund requests
- Record all actions
- Protect customer and seller data

---

# Mobile Requirements

The refund process must be optimized for smartphones.

---

# Accessibility

Support keyboard navigation and screen readers.

---

# RTL Support

Support Arabic RTL layouts.

---

# Localization

Supported languages:

- Arabic
- French
- English
- Spanish

---

# Conclusion

The refund flow should provide a transparent, fair, and secure process for resolving refund requests.
