# Purpose

This document defines the complete seller journey on Maroc Local Hub.

The goal is to provide sellers, cooperatives, and artisans with a simple, secure, and efficient way to manage their online business.

---

# Flow Overview

Seller Journey:

Home Page
↓

Register as Seller
↓

Email Verification
↓

Complete Seller Profile
↓

Create Store
↓

Add Products
↓

Publish Products
↓

Receive Orders
↓

Process Orders
↓

Ship Products
↓

Receive Payment
↓

Manage Customer Reviews

---

# Step 1: Seller Registration

The seller creates a new account.

Required information:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Password
- Seller Type

Supported seller types:

- Individual Seller
- Cooperative
- Artisan
- Company

---

# Step 2: Email Verification

The system sends a verification email.

The seller must verify the account before creating a store.

---

# Step 3: Complete Seller Profile

The seller completes:

- Profile Photo
- Business Name
- Business Description
- Address
- Country
- City
- Contact Information

---

# Step 4: Create Store

The seller creates an online store.

Store information includes:

- Store Name
- Store Logo
- Store Banner
- Store Description
- Product Categories

---

# Step 5: Add Products

The seller adds products with:

- Product Name
- Description
- Images
- Price
- Stock Quantity
- Category
- Shipping Information

---

# Step 6: Publish Products

Products are published after validation.

Published products become visible to customers.

---

# Step 7: Receive Orders

The seller receives notifications for new orders.

Available actions:

- Accept Order
- Reject Order
- Contact Customer

---

# Step 8: Process Orders

The seller prepares products for shipment.

The seller updates the order status.

Supported statuses:

- Pending
- Processing
- Ready to Ship
- Shipped
- Delivered

---

# Step 9: Shipping

The seller:

- Prints shipping label
- Confirms shipment
- Provides tracking number

Customers can track the shipment.

---

# Step 10: Payment

After successful delivery, the seller receives payment according to the platform's payment policy.

---

# Step 11: Reviews

Customers can leave reviews.

The seller may respond to customer reviews.

---

# Mobile Requirements

The entire seller experience must be optimized for smartphones.

---

# Accessibility

Support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- High color contrast

---

# RTL Support

The seller flow must fully support Arabic RTL layouts.

---

# Localization

Supported languages:

- Arabic
- French
- English
- Spanish

---

# Security

The seller system must:

- Verify seller identity
- Protect seller data
- Secure transactions
- Prevent unauthorized access

---

# Success Criteria

The seller should be able to:

- Register successfully
- Create a store
- Publish products
- Manage orders
- Receive payments
- Communicate with customers

---

# Conclusion

The Seller Flow should provide a secure, intuitive, and efficient experience for all sellers on Maroc Local Hub, helping them grow their business and reach customers locally and internationally.
