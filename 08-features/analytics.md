# Analytics Feature Specification

Document ID: MLH-FEAT-016
Version: 1.0.0
Status: Draft
Owner: Maroc Local Hub

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Analytics feature for Maroc Local Hub.

The analytics system provides insights into platform performance, user behavior, sales, and business growth.

---

# Objectives

The Analytics feature should:

- Monitor platform performance
- Track sales and revenue
- Analyze user behavior
- Support business decisions
- Generate reports

---

# Dashboard Metrics

The analytics dashboard should display:

- Total Users
- Active Users
- Total Sellers
- Total Products
- Total Orders
- Revenue
- Conversion Rate
- Customer Satisfaction

---

# Sales Analytics

Track:

- Daily Sales
- Weekly Sales
- Monthly Sales
- Annual Sales
- Best Selling Products
- Best Performing Sellers

---

# Customer Analytics

Track:

- New Customers
- Returning Customers
- Customer Retention
- Purchase Frequency
- Average Order Value

---

# Product Analytics

Track:

- Product Views
- Product Clicks
- Wishlist Additions
- Cart Additions
- Product Conversion Rate

---

# Reports

Generate reports for:

- Sales
- Orders
- Payments
- Inventory
- Shipping
- Customer Activity

---

# Export Options

Support exporting reports as:

- PDF
- Excel
- CSV

---

# Security

The analytics system must:

- Protect sensitive business data
- Restrict access by user role
- Maintain audit logs

---

# Mobile Requirements

The feature must be optimized for smartphones and tablets.

---

# Accessibility

Support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- High color contrast

---

# RTL Support

Fully support Arabic RTL layouts.

---

# Localization

Supported languages:

- Arabic
- French
- English
- Spanish

---

# Future Enhancements

- AI Predictive Analytics
- Sales Forecasting
- Customer Segmentation
- Custom Dashboards

---

# Conclusion

The Analytics feature should provide accurate and actionable insights that help improve business performance and support strategic decision-making.
