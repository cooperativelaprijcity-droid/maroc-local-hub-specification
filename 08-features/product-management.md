# Product Management Feature Specification

Document ID: MLH-FEAT-004
Version: 1.0.0
Status: Draft
Owner: Maroc Local Hub

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Product Management feature for Maroc Local Hub.

It enables sellers to create, organize, update, and manage products efficiently while providing customers with accurate product information.

---

# Objectives

The Product Management feature should:

- Create new products
- Edit existing products
- Delete products
- Organize products into categories
- Improve product visibility
- Maintain accurate inventory information

---

# Product Information

Each product should include:

- Product Name
- Product Description
- Product Images
- Product Category
- SKU
- Brand
- Price
- Discount
- Stock Quantity
- Weight
- Dimensions
- Tags

---

# Product Media

The platform should support:

- Multiple Images
- Product Videos
- 360° Product View (Future)
- 3D Product Model (Future)

---

# Product Categories

Products should belong to one or more categories.

Example categories:

- Honey
- Argan Oil
- Cosmetics
- Traditional Food
- Handicrafts
- Clothing
- Agriculture

---

# Product Status

Supported statuses:

- Draft
- Pending Review
- Published
- Out of Stock
- Archived

---

# Product Actions

Sellers should be able to:

- Create Product
- Edit Product
- Duplicate Product
- Delete Product
- Publish Product
- Archive Product

---

# Search Optimization

Products should support:

- SEO-friendly URL
- Meta Title
- Meta Description
- Keywords

---

# Security

The system must:

- Validate product information
- Prevent unauthorized modifications
- Record product history
- Protect uploaded media

---

# Mobile Requirements

The feature must be optimized for smartphones.

---

# Accessibility

Support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- High color contrast

---

# RTL Support

Fully support Arabic RTL layouts.

---

# Localization

Supported languages:

- Arabic
- French
- English
- Spanish

---

# Analytics

Track:

- Product Views
- Product Clicks
- Conversion Rate
- Best Selling Products
- Inventory Alerts

---

# Future Enhancements

- AI Product Description
- AI Image Enhancement
- AI Product Translation
- Automatic Category Suggestions

---

# Conclusion

The Product Management feature should provide sellers with a powerful and user-friendly system for managing products while ensuring high-quality product information across the marketplace.
